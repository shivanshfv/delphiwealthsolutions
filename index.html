The Idea

Before any decision of consequence, the ancients went to Delphi. Not for someone to act on their behalf, but for counsel clear enough to act on themselves.

That is the service we have rebuilt. Delphi Wealth Solutions exists for people who want a plan rather than a pitch: individuals, families, and business owners. Every recommendation is explained in plain terms, what it does, what it costs, and what it does not do, before any product is discussed.

What We Do

Three pillars. One standard.

I.

Personal & Family Protection

The foundation of the house: life and income protection, mortgage and family continuity, long-term care, health and Medicare guidance, and the estate documents that tie it all together.

II.

Wealth & Tax Strategy

A clear map of where your retirement money sits across the three tax buckets: taxed now, taxed later, tax advantaged. Retirement income design, annuity suitability, and distribution modeling, run on your actual numbers.

III.

Business Owner Solutions

The business you built needs its own plan, separate from your personal one: buy-sell agreements, key person protection, executive bonus and retention plans, and owner retirement design.

What We Don’t Do

We do not manage portfolios.

This is deliberate. Freed from managing assets, we have no incentive to gather them, which means our advice answers to your interests rather than to a fee on your balance.

Your investment managers, accountants, and attorneys keep their roles. We make sure they’re all rowing in the same direction.
